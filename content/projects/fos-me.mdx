## Overview

FOS ME addresses the challenge of managing food-ordering operations inside one tightly coupled application. The team developed a modular microservices platform with centralized routing and authentication.

## Architecture

- **API Gateway:** centralized request routing and authentication across services
- **Services:** independently managed food-ordering capabilities
- **Frontend:** Next.js
- **Backend:** Node.js and Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB

The team delivered the system through an accelerated four-week development and testing cycle, focusing on reliability, faster iteration, and a structure suitable for high-traffic food delivery workflows.
